# Code of Conduct

This is a small hobby project for people who play LET IT DIE Offline. Everyone is welcome to use it, report problems and suggest improvements.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are not programmers, and some are editing a save for the first time.
- Stay on topic and be specific when you report a problem.
- Respect other people's choices. How someone plays their own game is up to them.
- Give and accept feedback politely. Criticise the work, never the person.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Posting other people's private information.
- Sexual or violent content, spam, or advertising.
- Asking for, or sharing, help to cheat other players. This editor is for offline saves only.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@oberlinx](https://github.com/oberlinx), through GitHub. If you don't want to post it publicly, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or send the maintainer a private message through GitHub.
